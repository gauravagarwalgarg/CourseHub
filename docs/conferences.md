## 🎯 How to Watch Conference Talks Effectively

1. **Pick 2-3 conferences** relevant to your domain
2. **Watch 1 talk per day** during lunch or commute (most are 30-45 min)
3. **Take notes** one sentence per key insight
4. **Implement something** from each talk within a week
5. **Share** teach someone else what you learned
