## 🎯 The Engineer's Life Checklist

```
□ Emergency fund (6 months expenses in FD/liquid fund)
□ Term insurance (if you have dependents)
□ Health insurance (beyond employer's)
□ Start SIP in index fund (even ₹5000/month)
□ Max out 80C (₹1.5L: ELSS/PPF/NPS)
□ NPS for extra ₹50K deduction (Section 80CCD)
□ No lifestyle inflation with every raise
□ Learn to say no (to meetings, to scope creep, to burnout)
□ Exercise 30 min/day (non-negotiable)
□ Read 1 book/month (not just tech)
□ Write publicly (blog, Twitter, LinkedIn)
□ Build one side project per year
□ Network: attend 2 meetups/conferences per year
□ Negotiate every offer (never accept first number)
□ Review career goals every 6 months
```
